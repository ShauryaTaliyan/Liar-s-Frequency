# Liar's Frequency — Visual Novel Artwork Requests

The current project is now organized as a scene-first visual novel. The approved home image and opening-cinematic images must remain untouched. The existing amber desk, notebook, podcast-studio, grunge, and golden-seam assets can remain as transitional/background art. These are the next custom assets that will make character scenes and evidence feel like a real graphic novel.

## Global style lock — include in every image prompt

Minimalist 2D noir graphic-novel illustration; crisp deliberate black ink contours; flat, hand-painted color blocks; extreme chiaroscuro; predominantly pure black shadows; restrained amber/golden practical light; cinematic framing; subtle paper/ink texture; no photorealism; no 3D render; no glossy UI; no text, captions, logos, watermarks, or interface elements. Keep all characters visually consistent in proportions, clothing, line thickness, and amber lighting.

## Batch 1 — Character art (8 images)

For all character images: PNG with a transparent background, upper-body / waist-up, 3:4 portrait composition, the full head and shoulders visible, facing slightly toward the viewer's left or right so the character can be placed opposite dialogue. No labels or text. Keep a clean outer silhouette so the art can be layered over a scene background.

### 1. Anika Rao — investigative partner

An Indian woman in her early-to-mid 30s, sharp observant eyes, dark shoulder-length hair, charcoal blazer over a simple dark top. Expression: impatient but focused, like she has been waiting too long and needs the detective to concentrate. Professional, composed posture. Warm amber rim-light on one side; the other side falls into solid black shadow. This is the recurring portrait used for partner dialogue.

Filename suggestion: `anika-rao-neutral.png`

### 2. Meera Sethi — guarded

An Indian woman, 34, corporate lawyer, refined but understated appearance, tailored dark blazer, hair carefully arranged. Expression: guarded and articulate, holding back personal information without looking villainous. Keep lips relaxed and eyes alert. Amber light catches her cheek and blazer edge; background fully transparent.

Filename suggestion: `meera-sethi-guarded.png`

### 3. Meera Sethi — defensive

The *same exact character design, face, hair, age, blazer, camera angle, proportions, and lighting* as the guarded Meera reference. Expression only changes: jaw tense, eyes briefly averted, controlled distress as she admits the argument with Vikram. Transparent background.

Filename suggestion: `meera-sethi-defensive.png`

### 4. Arjun Malhotra — composed

An Indian man, 40, polished CFO, neatly styled dark hair, charcoal business suit, crisp shirt, no flashy accessories. Expression: calm, precise, professionally cooperative, almost too controlled. Strong silhouette, subtle amber edge-light, black ink facial shadows. Transparent background.

Filename suggestion: `arjun-malhotra-composed.png`

### 5. Arjun Malhotra — cornered

The *same exact Arjun design* as the composed reference: same face, hair, suit, age, camera angle, and lighting. Expression only changes: jaw tight, brow slightly pinched, composure beginning to crack while he tries not to show panic. Avoid theatrical anger or a cartoon villain expression. Transparent background.

Filename suggestion: `arjun-malhotra-cornered.png`

### 6. Dev Bhatia — nervous

An Indian man, 48, building security supervisor in a simple dark navy uniform with a small generic badge (no legible text or logo), tired eyes, visible lines around the mouth. Expression: nervous and defensive, as if worried about losing his job. Amber light from a monitor illuminates one side of the face. Transparent background.

Filename suggestion: `dev-bhatia-nervous.png`

### 7. Dev Bhatia — admitting the camera outage

The *same exact Dev design* as the nervous reference: same face, uniform, age, proportions, and lighting. Expression only changes: eyes lowered, shoulders slightly drawn inward, reluctant admission and shame rather than guilt for murder. Transparent background.

Filename suggestion: `dev-bhatia-admitting.png`

### 8. Vikram Sethi — victim portrait

An Indian man, 38, technology entrepreneur and founder of a successful company, composed and intelligent, dark tailored suit, understated expensive watch, confident but tired eyes. A neutral professional portrait with subtle amber rim light and deep shadow. This appears in the case file, meeting draft, and later flashback / recollection scenes. Transparent background preferred; no text or frame.

Filename suggestion: `vikram-sethi-portrait.png`

## Batch 2 — Story backgrounds (3 images)

For all backgrounds: 16:9 landscape, ideally 1920×1080 or larger. Preserve strong negative space along the lower 25% for the visual-novel dialogue box. No readable text, labels, UI, logos, or watermarks.

### 9. Vikram's executive office — crime-scene overview

A modern technology-company executive office at night, floor-to-ceiling windows with a dark city beyond, broad desk, one overturned chair, a few glass fragments near the desk, an expensive watch and laptop left in place. One hard amber light cuts across the room while the corners remain almost pure black. No body visible and no graphic injury. Frame it like a police evidence photograph reinterpreted as a 2D noir graphic novel; keep room geometry easy to understand.

Filename suggestion: `sethi-executive-office.png`

### 10. Executive corridor — camera blind spot

A long empty corporate corridor at night, one visible CCTV camera mounted high near the ceiling, office doors along one side, faint amber emergency lighting, deep shadows and converging perspective. A small patch of the corridor is unlit to suggest the camera gap. No people, no signage, no readable text. Leave the lower area dark for dialogue.

Filename suggestion: `executive-corridor-cctv.png`

### 11. Security monitoring room

A cramped building security room with a desk, several CCTV monitors showing abstract monochrome corridor views (no legible timestamps or UI text), one empty chair, and a single amber desk lamp. Noir ink contours, black negative space, restricted amber highlights. The scene should feel like a place where Dev spent the night, not a futuristic sci-fi control room.

Filename suggestion: `security-monitoring-room.png`

## Batch 3 — Evidence close-ups (4 images)

These are used as individual evidence reveal shots, not as interface screenshots. 16:9 landscape, high contrast, no legible generated text. Real evidence wording will be rendered by the game UI on top of the illustration.

### 12. Vendor payment ledger

A close-up of an open finance ledger and a printed payment report under a hard amber desk lamp, a few repeated transfers visually emphasized by pencil circles or tabs, fountain pen and folded meeting note nearby. No readable numbers or words. The implication should be suspicious financial activity, not a clear confession.

Filename suggestion: `evidence-vendor-ledger.png`

### 13. Access badge and terminal

A plain corporate access badge beside a small access-control reader and a printed activity strip with abstract, unreadable rows. Deep black shadows, golden reflection on the badge edge. Do not show a real company logo or legible data.

Filename suggestion: `evidence-access-record.png`

### 14. The 9:17 call

A close-up of a smartphone lying on a dark desk, its screen showing a generic call-history layout with one highlighted recent call, but absolutely no legible text, real contact names, or numbers. A faint amber light illuminates the edge of the phone; keep the screen dark enough for the UI to overlay the actual time and call duration.

Filename suggestion: `evidence-call-record.png`

### 15. Camera maintenance record

A small maintenance clipboard and a CCTV control panel with one indicator light switched off, lit by a narrow amber beam. Abstract unreadable log marks only, no words. This image represents Dev's manual camera shutdown without depicting it as proof of murder.

Filename suggestion: `evidence-camera-log.png`

## Upload order

Start with **Batch 1 (images 1–8)** so recurring characters have consistent designs. Generate Meera's and Arjun's neutral portraits first, then use each as a visual reference for the matching expression image. After those, create the three backgrounds (9–11), then evidence close-ups (12–15).

Do not combine several character designs into one collage or sprite sheet; each image should be a separate file. Keep the same visual-style lock throughout the set. The supplied approved home-screen and opening-cinematic assets are not to be regenerated or modified.
